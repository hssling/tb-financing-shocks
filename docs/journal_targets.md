# Journal targets, verified 15 November 2026

The author's rule: hybrid or free to publish; no article-processing charge. Each target below was checked
against the journal's own pages before any formatting work (an earlier choice, Health Policy and
Planning, was made without checking and turned out to be fully open access with a mandatory charge).

## Paper C → Bulletin of the World Health Organization (Research)

Source: <https://www.who.int/publications/journals/bulletin/contributors/guidelines-for-contributors>

- Fees: "The Bulletin is fully open-access and charges no author fees." Qualifies.
- Research: at most 3,000 words and 50 references; structured abstract (Objective, Methods, Findings,
  Conclusion), at most 250 words. Word limits exclude abstract, tables, boxes, figures, references.
- Required: two paragraphs on what the paper adds to the literature; ICMJE recommendations with any AI
  use disclosed; EQUATOR reporting guideline; competing interests and funding statements.
- References numbered consecutively, in parentheses or superscript, ICMJE style.
- Submission: <http://submit.bwho.org>.

## Paper D → International Journal of Tuberculosis and Lung Disease (Original Article)

Sources: IJTLD Instructions for Authors 2025 (theunion.org, PDF); journal publication charges page
<https://theunion.org/our-work/journals/ijtld/information-for-authors/journal-publication-charges>.

- Fees: no publication fee within length limits. Excess pages over six cost €380 each; hosting
  supplementary data costs €350 per 10 pages in 2026; open access is no longer offered in IJTLD (Coalition S).
  **So: stay within six typeset pages and keep supplementary material in the code deposit, not with the
  journal.**
- Original Article: 6 typeset pages, about 2,500 words (excluding abstract, references, tables, figure
  captions), structured summary 200 words, up to 5 tables/figures, 35 references.
- Reporting: STROBE for epidemiological studies; Uniform Requirements (ICMJE).
- Submission: <http://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/ijtld>.
